# Miggoo — What's Happening Now?

Students don't miss events because events don't exist — they miss them because discovery is broken. Miggoo is a map-first, real-time event discovery app that solves this by showing what's happening **right now** on your campus, scored by time-relevance and social proof, so the "magical moment" — opening the app and seeing something worth leaving home for — actually happens. Unlike social feeds or group chats, Miggoo's core loop is proximity over virality and discovery over engagement: a dark-themed live campus map with pulsing neon markers for active events, per-event chat, community groups, and user connections — everything a student needs to go from "what's going on?" to "I'm heading there" in under 10 seconds. The built-in ambassador dashboard with batch seeding tools and north-star metric tracking (active visible events per user per week) ensures campus-by-campus liquidity before growth, making the cold-start problem an operational playbook rather than a prayer.
