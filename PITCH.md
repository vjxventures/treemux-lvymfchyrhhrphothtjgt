# Miggoo — What's happening now?

Miggoo solves the invisible campus problem: thousands of events happen every week on university campuses, but students miss 90% of them because discovery depends on closed group chats and ephemeral stories. Miggoo is a map-first, real-time event discovery app that shows you what's happening *right now* near you — live events pulse on a dark-themed map, upcoming ones appear by proximity and time, and you can join, chat, and connect with one tap.

What makes Miggoo different from event boards and social platforms is its obsession with the *now-moment*: the app is designed so that within 3 seconds of opening it, you see something interesting happening nearby and feel compelled to leave your room. It's built around campus trust (university email verification, ambassador moderation, community-driven event creation) rather than algorithmic engagement — because belonging matters more than scale.

The MVP ships with a complete map-based discovery UI with live event markers, category filtering, event creation with vibe tags, campus communities with group chat, user profiles with peer connections, and an ambassador dashboard for moderation — all backed by a SQLite database that seeds automatically with realistic MIT campus data, demonstrating what a thriving campus network looks like from day one.
